# How to Add Your Images to the Portfolio

## 📁 Folder Structure

Create an `images` folder in your portfolio directory with the following structure:

```
portfolio/
├── index.html
├── styles.css
├── script.js
├── images/              ← Create this folder
│   ├── profile1.jpg     ← Your first photo (standing photo)
│   ├── profile2.jpg     ← Your second photo (library photo)
│   ├── library-management.jpg    ← Your Library Management System design
│   ├── game-ui.jpg      ← Your Game UI design
│   ├── web-design.jpg   ← Your web design projects
│   ├── mobile-app.jpg   ← Your mobile app designs
│   ├── branding.jpg     ← Your branding projects
│   └── dashboard.jpg    ← Your dashboard designs
```

## 🖼️ Image Requirements

### Profile Photos (Already Provided by You):
1. **profile1.jpg** - Use the first image you provided (standing photo)
2. **profile2.jpg** - Use the second image you provided (library photo)

### Portfolio/Design Images:
For your design showcase, you'll need to add images of your actual design work:

1. **library-management.jpg** - Screenshot of your Library Management System UI from Dribbble
2. **game-ui.jpg** - Screenshot of your Children's Game UI from Danson Solution internship

5. **branding.jpg** - Screenshot of any branding/logo work

## 📝 Steps to Add Images:

### Step 1: Create the images folder
1. Open File Explorer
2. Navigate to: `C:\Users\rashmee\OneDrive\Documents\portfolio`
3. Create a new folder named `images`

### Step 2: Add Your Profile Photos
1. Save the two photos I provided above as:
   - `profile1.jpg` (standing photo)
   - `profile2.jpg` (library/bookshelf photo)
2. Copy them into the `images` folder

### Step 3: Add Your Design Work
1. Take screenshots of your designs from:
   - Your Behance portfolio
   - Your Dribbble shots
   - Your design files in Figma
   - Any other design work you want to showcase
2. Save them with the names mentioned above
3. Copy them into the `images` folder

## 💡 Tips:

- **Image Size**: Recommended size is 1200x800px for portfolio images
- **File Format**: Use JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Try to keep each image under 500KB for fast loading
- **Quality**: Use high-quality images that showcase your work clearly

## 🚀 Alternative: Use Placeholder Images

If you don't have all design images ready, you can:
1. Start with just the profile photos
2. The portfolio section will show a colored background until you add the design images
3. Add design images gradually as you collect them

## ✨ Once Images Are Added:

Simply refresh your browser (F5) and your portfolio will display:
- Your actual photos in the Hero and About sections
- Your design work in the Portfolio section
